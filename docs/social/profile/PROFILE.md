## Facebook Page

**1. Create the page.** On facebook.com (from your personal profile): Menu → **Pages** → **Create new Page**.

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Page name | `NukkadSale` (Facebook rejects taglines in page names, so the tagline goes in the bio) |
| Category (up to 3) | **App Page**, **Software Company** |
| Bio / Intro (101-character limit) | `Sell on WhatsApp, the easy way. Order links, exact-amount UPI, auto khata. हिंदी + English.` (91 characters) |

**2. Images.**
- Profile picture: `profile-picture.png`.
- Cover photo: `facebook-cover.png`. After uploading, drag it so it's centred, then check it once on your phone.

**3. Details** (Edit Page → Contact info / Details):
- Website: `https://nukkadsale.in`
- Email: your support email
- Phone / WhatsApp: your **support** number
- Location: none needed (online business). Service area: **India**.
- Hours: **No hours available**, or your real support hours, e.g. Mon–Sat 10 am–7 pm.
- Username: same as Instagram (Edit Page → Username).

**4. Action button.** **+ Add action button** → **Sign up** → website `https://nukkadsale.in/signup`. Alternative: **Send WhatsApp message** to your support number.

**5. About / description** (Edit Page → About → Description):

> NukkadSale helps sellers who sell in WhatsApp groups take orders without the DM chaos.
>
> • Post a sale with photos, price and sizes. NukkadSale writes the WhatsApp message for your groups.
> • Buyers tap your post and order right inside WhatsApp. The ordering bot asks size and address.
> • Every order gets its own exact amount (like ₹798.79 for a ₹799 kurta), so you know who paid. Buyers pay you directly on UPI. Big sellers can switch to Razorpay, where payments confirm themselves.
> • Stock is held for the buyer while they pay, so the last piece isn't sold twice.
> • Pack & send, buyer list and a khata that fills itself, all in one app on your phone. हिंदी + English.
>
> NukkadSale never holds your money – payments go straight to you.
> Start selling: nukkadsale.in

**6. Connect Instagram and Facebook.** Facebook Page → Settings → **Linked accounts** → Instagram → Connect. Then use **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) to post once to both and to answer comments and DMs from one inbox.

**7. Pinned post.** Post Reel 6 first, then on the Page: ⋯ on the post → **Pin to featured**.

## Note: which WhatsApp number goes on the pages

Put your **support** number (the one in the go-live doc, Step 3) on Instagram and Facebook, **not** the bot's ordering number. Messages to the bot number are handled by the ordering bot, so a seller writing "hi, how do I sign up?" there would get a bot reply instead of you.
