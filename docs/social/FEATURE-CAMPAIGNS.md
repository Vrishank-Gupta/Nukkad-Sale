## 1 · Discount on a listed sale

- **Reel – "Sale lagao. 10 seconds."** · *look:* bright yellow + black price-tag graphics (new palette).
  Hook frame 0: a big price tag "₹1,000" gets slashed to "₹800" with a red stroke. → seller taps **Discount →
  20% off → Start discount** (real screen) → buyer page with ₹800, ₹1,000 struck, "20% off" (real) → the
  WhatsApp message with ~₹1,000~ → "Orders already placed keep their price." End: "Sale lagao, sale badhao."
- **Post (single image)** – before/after split: left "₹1,000", right "₹800 · 20% off" on the real buyer page.
  Caption: how to run a weekend sale on an item that isn't moving.
- **Stories (3 frames, add to "How it works" highlight later)** – where the Discount button is → pick 20% → what
  buyers see.
- *New screens to capture:* seller sale page with the Discount panel open; buyer page with a discount.

## 3 · Restock waitlist

- **Stories** – reshare + a quiz: "What do you do when an item sells out? Restock / Move on / Make a new sale".
