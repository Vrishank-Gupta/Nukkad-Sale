## Momentum mechanics

- **Repurpose everything 3 ways**: reel → cover image as a post → frames as a story series.
- **Every post goes to stories** the same day; every story series goes to a highlight.
- **Comments are the idea engine**: the question post and question box answers become next week's reels.
- **Collab posts** with 2–3 small reseller/boutique accounts (Instagram "Invite collaborator") once they use
  the app – real shops, real permission.
- **Boost only winners**: after 48 h, boost the post/reel with the best saves + shares, not the most likes.
- **Track weekly**: reach, saves, shares, profile visits, link taps, "Get started" form fills (Admin →
  Requests). Double down on the bucket that drives form fills.
